The result that matters

A long sign required 131 generated tokens. Our 96-token cap truncated an otherwise correct reading. Raising the allowance to 512 and requiring an end-of-sequence token recovered its complete text. All 91 earlier normalized outputs remained unchanged. These are small public-photo experiments containing repeated and development-influenced sources, not an independent accuracy estimate.

Inspect the executable evidence

The video build calls the published native_reader.finish() function with two explicit token fixtures: an unfinished 96-token sequence must be rejected, while a 131-token sequence ending with EOS is accepted. The tests do not execute a neural model.
