01 / THE FAILURE

Approval is not a receipt.

An event can be eligible at enqueue and orphaned at dispatch. A receiver can commit while its acknowledgement is lost. Neither rolling back an index nor blindly retrying resolves both cases.

02 / THE BUILD

A real worker. A separate receiver.

Compiled Solidity fixture and pinned EVM observations → shared eligibility engine → SQLite outbox → loopback HTTP receiver → separate ticket ledger. Restart the worker and reconcile its receipt.

03 / THE DIFFERENCE

Reproduce the failure.
Keep the outside effect.

Same waiting rule, same order: the enqueue-only consumer issues one orphaned fixture ticket; Forkline holds the command. If a ticket already exists, retain its receipt, latch the incident and stop further guarded delivery.
