    How the checks work, and what they do not establish
    Counterstep compares real solution sets using an exact rational parser. A second check requires the requested step's resulting left/right coefficients, allowing equation-side swaps. Expansion must remove brackets; arithmetic must produce a numeric literal. This rejects a copied question or a final answer when expansion was requested, even if the solution set is unchanged.
    The unchanged neural model chooses among five authored practice families. The new changed-structure questions do not expand the classifier's validation: its existing fresh numeric test was 487/600 top-class matches within its original five generators. No teacher review, learner trial, calibrated diagnosis or measured learning gain is claimed.
    Supported: 2–12 linear equations in x, constants, rational numbers and parentheses. No nonlinear products, variable denominators, inequalities or other variables. The repair path requires a unique solution. A valid equation chain cannot establish unwritten reasoning or understanding.
    Saved files replay inputs and help requests; saved outcome claims are ignored. Fingerprints identify bytes, not authorship. A file holder can construct another valid history, and the generators are in the source. This is a personal practice tool, not an exam or authenticated grade.
