Read the dispatch assumptions

One shared machine, up to four non-overlapping jobs and 24 hourly slots. Every job must finish by its deadline. Power values are slot averages; surge current is not modeled.

Solar supplies load first. Excess solar charges the battery. Above-reserve battery energy covers deficits before the grid. The model does not charge from the grid or save battery strategically for a later outage.

Inverter/AC load limit is checked against total demand even when grid is available. Charge and discharge rate limits and conversion losses are included.

The search is exhaustive within this fixed-dispatch model unless it hits its time or evaluation limit. It is not globally optimal energy management or electrical-design advice.
