Cut the rest.

A clip can quote every word correctly and still omit the sentence that changes its meaning. CutProof finds useful source ranges, shows what a cut leaves out, and exports real captioned clips with an inspectable editing record.

Try the differentiating workflow in a minute.

  1. Open the studio and select Try a risky cut. The source words are exact, but the cut omits an invented setup and a qualification.
  2. Inspect Boundary Lab. Added original sentences are highlighted next to the short excerpt.
  3. Choose Use wider source range. The words are not rewritten, and review remains pending.
  4. Play the selection, review the matching video, then render a captioned WebM or export the editing ZIP.

To run automatic selection instead, select Load built-in demo, then Find source-linked clips. Your own timestamped captions and matching video work through the same controls.

Source links are not truth.

English lexical checks can miss sarcasm, distant qualifications, visual context, and other nuance. A transcript fingerprint verifies integrity, not agreement with the recording.

There is no speech transcription, pretrained semantic model, engagement prediction, or independent creator time-saving study in this build.

Inspect the evidence, including failures.

The source is rebuilt in a separate execution environment. The pipeline stops on failed tests and publishes its actual result. Internal fixtures are not an independent audit or general detection-accuracy benchmark.
