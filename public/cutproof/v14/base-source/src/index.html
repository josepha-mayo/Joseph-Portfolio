From source to a real handoff.

Import. Open a timestamped SRT, VTT, or JSON transcript. Attach the video from the same recording. No transcription model runs here.

Select. The local ranker uses corpus TF-IDF, sentence structure, and diversity. Selections contain whole, consecutive source cues. English lexical checks flag possible missing context.

Review. Inspect the earlier and later cues in Source trace. Change boundaries when needed. Review states start as pending and reset when boundaries or the attached video change. Boundary Lab shows the omitted words and proposes an expanded range. Saved projects restore with all reviews pending until the original video is reattached and checked.

Export. The ZIP includes a manifest, source cues, captions, verbatim publication drafts, and a Python MP4 renderer. In supported browsers, Render WebM records a portrait version locally in real time. Keep the tab visible during recording.

No paid service, pretrained model, cloud upload, auto-publication, or claim of guaranteed engagement is included. Source alignment is not proof of truth.
