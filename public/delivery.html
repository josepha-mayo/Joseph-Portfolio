Boundaries that matter

A reorg after dispatch can still orphan a ticket. The worker retains its receipt and pauses; it cannot take the ticket back. Idempotency is provided by this receiver, not by every external API.

The lab trusts block-hash-pinned local-EVM observations. Confirmation waits are not consensus finality. It is a single-worker test adapter, not a production security review or multi-worker queue.

Raw SQLite files and a separate Python ledger check are included in the executed evidence. Synthetic orders only; no measured user or financial outcome.
