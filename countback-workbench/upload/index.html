Inspect the evidence.
Not just the result screen.

Select reference close-ups and group photos. Countback runs OpenCV on those actual files, then opens a review with the available evidence and uncertainty intact.

Processing happens on this computer. No AWS call, cloud upload, account or automatic kit approval.

1

Reference items

One close-up per reference. To leave background out, drag a rectangle around the object in its preview. Only the reference crop is supplied; group-photo locations are not.

2

Group photographs

Choose up to three views of the same arrangement. The first is inspected first; later photographs are processed only when the current evidence leaves items unresolved.

    Repeated photos do not count as additional evidence. JPEG or PNG, at most 1.5 MB per file, 3.6 MB total.

    The private Review Desk includes display copies of your images. Its “Export handoff” button exports the decisions and fingerprints without photo pixels. Human assessments start pending. Display redaction is not a general sensitive-text detector.

    Local development service, not an internet-facing deployment. One analysis at a time, with a 90-second worker limit. Temporary upload files are removed after processing.
