## Interpretation limits

Prize-package valuations are not liquid cash. VoltHacks and UnivaBio itemize service benefits without a separate cash component; GIBC and Galuxium explicitly describe non-cash awards. GatewayHacks lists $50/$25/$10 cash despite much larger package valuations. Galuxium also discloses a paid subscription prerequisite for part of the credit redemption.

OpenCV has a 14-minute discrepancy: its configured close is October 26 at 23:45 Pacific, while prose says 23:59. The earlier time appears above. An open registration form does not resolve its earlier-proposal condition. Its submission configuration requires a video and ZIP.

Amazon cash and credits are separate. Its rules state: "A project can only win one (1) track prize and one (1) mini challenge prize." The rules describe prize payment after required forms, not an immediate payment on announcement.

AI Builders has a mandatory Discord announcement and an unresolved $4,000-versus-TBD discrepancy. AI Content Engine is already judging; a shared project should not be casually overwritten while a closed entry is under review.
