01 / The job

Examples are synthetic bills of materials, not measured savings.

Every detached piece consumes one saw kerf, including the last piece. End trim includes its own cut loss. This is deliberately conservative; verify material quality and measurements yourself.

Batch search groups equal lengths, not physical pieces. Up to 120 pieces and 8 distinct lengths. A checked plan can be usable without being proven optimal.

Downloading the Python runtime once. Planning will then work without a network connection while this page stays open.

04 / Nothing disappears

Finished pieces + saw kerf + end trim + kept tails + short tails must equal used stock.

Objective, in order: minimum new length purchased, minimum modeled scrap, then fewest bars cut. A reusable tail is not counted as waste prevented.

Decision support, not machine control. One-dimensional cut planning only; no knots, defects, grain, two-dimensional nesting, stock prices or carbon accounting. Original project for PyStorm, September 2026. Built with AI assistance.
